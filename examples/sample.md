# Colour management programme

The Centre for Digital Behaviour organises its work around a simple licence
model. Members practise what they preach: every programme is analysed, every
storey of the building is catalogued, and "colour" is spelt the British way --
whilst our American colleagues favour 'color'.

## Practical details

- The licence fee is 10 pounds and the practice runs 3-5 sessions a week.
- Please send your cheque to the Labour Party office by 4 September 2026.
- The theatre programme lists the metre readings, the tyre pressures, and the
  disc brakes we've organised for the aluminium trailer.
- As the Defence Ministry's report put it, "the colour scheme was unauthorised".

The program crashed after the check cleared, and the story about the storey
made us tire of the whole thing. It's the Joneses' problem now, towards the end
of the '90s.
